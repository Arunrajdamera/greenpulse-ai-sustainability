# 2–3 minute demonstration

1. Open **Dashboard** and point out the `Demo Dataset – Simulated Resource Data` badge, trends and priority cards.
2. Open **Resource Analysis**, choose Electricity and click **Analyze resource**. Show Block A’s recent value, baseline, difference and why it was flagged.
3. Open **AI Assistant**, use the prefilled question about sudden electricity use and click **Ask assistant**. Point to the Demo AI Mode label and listed local sources.
4. Open **Recommendations**, click **Generate recommendations**, and narrate the four workflow stages and Pending Review status.
5. Open **Responsible AI** to explain simulated data, grounding and human oversight.
6. Finish at **SDG Alignment**, highlighting SDG 12 and secondary SDGs 7, 11 and 13.
