# Energy conservation
Electricity consumption in a campus building can rise when HVAC or air-conditioning operates for longer, lighting is left on, equipment and plug loads increase, operating hours are extended, occupancy changes, or unusual equipment is used. These are general possibilities, not claims about a particular campus. Energy use in learning spaces can be reduced by switching off lights, displays, fans and computers when rooms are unoccupied. Facility teams should review HVAC and lighting schedules, investigate unexpected baseloads, and verify equipment condition. Start with observation and meter checks before changing operations.

# SDG 7 and 13
Efficient energy use supports SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) and can support climate-conscious behaviour under SDG 13. Improvements should be measured locally rather than assumed.
